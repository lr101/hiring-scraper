Task: tailor the CV to this posting so a recruiter scanning it sees the posting's core duties and
key terms, backed by the applicant's verified evidence. Preserve verified employers, titles, dates
and section order. The applicant reviews every changed unit next to its source before sending.

How your answer is used
- The application builds the CV from the base CV and your slots: it keeps the base layout and
  sections, swaps in your wording, renders the PDF and shows each changed unit as an "AI change"
  that the applicant accepts, edits or restores.
- Checks that reject the whole draft: a number not in the unit's cited sources, a citation of
  another entry's evidence, a scope qualifier not in the cited evidence (see Truthfulness), first
  person in the profile, a headline over FORMAT.headline.max_chars, or a CV longer than two pages.
- Checks shown to the applicant as review notes: tools or terms not found in the cited evidence or
  profile skills, a rewrite that drops a detail of its base bullet, four-word phrases repeated
  between bullets of one entry, an opening verb starting more than two bullets, profile sentences
  over 28 words, and gaps you report.

Targets
- TARGETS lists the posting's duties and requirements, quoted from JOB by the matcher (`need`:
  must or nice). When TARGETS has no items, derive them from JOB.
- Each target's key terms are what this CV should show where the evidence supports them: use the
  posting's own words (e.g. "Prozessoptimierung", "Lean", "Power BI") instead of near synonyms, so
  the applicant's verified work reads in the posting's language.

Format
- FORMAT describes the base CV's headline and profile. Keep that format and change only the
  wording: the same structure, separator, number of parts, sentence count and similar length.

Headline
- Return: when FORMAT.headline has `segments`, exactly that many parts in `headline.segments`;
  otherwise one line no longer than FORMAT.headline.max_chars. Cite the supporting evidence ids.
- Write: short noun phrases naming 2–3 verified functional pillars, ordered by fit for the
  posting's core duties, in the posting's role-family wording where it accurately describes
  verified work. No unheld job title; no single study project promoted to a specialty.
- Used: the parts are joined with the base separator and replace the base headline.

Profile
- Return: `profile.first` … `profile.fourth` in order, exactly FORMAT.profile.sentences sentences
  (unused ones null), about FORMAT.profile.words words in total, each with its own `sources` from
  one entry plus general/skill evidence.
- Write: lead with a verified professional identity, connect to the strongest evidence for the
  posting's must-have targets using their key terms, and add distinct technical or analytical
  strengths. Synthesize rather than repeat bullet wording. Subject-less CV style: no "I"/"ich",
  no "she"/"he"/"Die Ingenieurin"; open noun-led (e.g. "Ingenieurin für … mit Erfahrung in …").
  Study projects are study context; a company in a project label is a topic unless evidence states
  an employer or client relationship. Leave language levels to their section and motivation to the
  letter.
- Used: the sentences fill the base profile's paragraphs in order.

Entries (one slot per base bullet)
- Input: ENTRIES gives each base bullet's `bullet_id`, `source` and text, grouped by entry.
- Return for every bullet: `relevance` (high, medium, low for the posting's targets), and per entry
  the `order` of all `bullet_id`s with the strongest evidence for the core duties first.
- Rewrite (return `text`, `source`, `support`, `keywords`) every high or medium bullet. Keep
  (return only `relevance`) low bullets, and a relevant bullet only when it already names its
  target's key terms in the posting's wording. Each rewrite must add at least one of: a posting key
  term for work the bullet shows, the result framed for the target duty, or a verified detail
  from `support`. Swapping synonyms alone is not a rewrite.
- `source` is the bullet's own base source and stays its main fact. `support` may add ids of
  this entry's intro, profile skill evidence (`skill:*`), career evidence (`evidence:*`) or
  certifications, only when that evidence describes work in this same role or employer. Add a
  support fact as its own clause or second sentence ("…; dazu …", "…, including …"), never as a
  cause or result of the bullet's fact. Never merge two base bullets or move facts between
  entries.
- Preserve the base bullet's actors, tools, numbers, results and substantive details. A rewrite may
  be up to about one and a half times the base length; stay within two pages. Vary opening verbs
  and omit any existing `lead_in` prefix from `text` (the application keeps it).
- `keywords`: the posting terms (as written in JOB or TARGETS) that your rewrite contains verbatim.
- Used: kept bullets stay verbatim; rewrites replace their base bullet and cite `source` plus
  `support`. The application verifies each keyword appears in the text.

Skills
- Return each skills section's existing `unit_ids` ordered by relevance to the targets; keep
  every item. Used: the items are reordered only.

Gaps
- Return in `gaps` each must-have (and notable nice-to-have) key term from TARGETS that no
  evidence supports, as a short term with its `need`. Never write unsupported terms into the CV.
- Used: each gap becomes a review note asking the applicant to add it only if it is true; gaps
  the CV already shows are dropped.
