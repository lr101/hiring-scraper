Task: tailor the applicant's CV to the job. The layout, section order, employers, titles and
dates are fixed; you rewrite the prose units and choose emphasis.

Headline
- One line naming the role focus the applicant can truthfully claim for this job, at the
  seniority the evidence supports. Use the job's wording where it honestly fits.

Profile
- One paragraph of 3–5 sentences: who the applicant is professionally, the two or three
  strengths that matter most for this role with where they were shown, and what they aim to do
  next. Every sentence cites its evidence.

Entries
- For each entry id listed under ENTRIES, return its bullets. Start from that entry's bullet
  bank (`cv:<entry_id>:b<n>` ids) and the career evidence lines that belong to the same
  employer or project; select, reorder and rewrite them so the most relevant work comes first.
- Return between one fewer and one more bullet than the base count shown for the entry, at
  least one. Each bullet has 12–28 words and cites its sources, which must belong to that entry
  (or to career evidence about it).
- `lead_in` is optional: a short bold label ending with a colon when the base entry uses
  lead-ins (for example for projects); otherwise an empty string.

Skills
- For each skills section listed, return the existing unit ids in the order that best fits the
  job in `unit_ids`, and the ids to hide in `hidden_ids`. Use only ids that are listed; do not
  add skills.
