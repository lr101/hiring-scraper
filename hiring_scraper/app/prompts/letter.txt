Task: write a cover letter for the specific work in the posting. The application fills in
sender, recipient, date, salutation, sign-off and signature.

Structure
- Write three body paragraphs and a short closing by default. Add a fourth body paragraph
  only if it proves another core requirement or offers a specific application of a verified
  method. Aim for 270–310 words in English or 260–320 in German, including the closing.
- Motivation: in two or three sentences, connect a specific problem or responsibility in the
  posting to the applicant's verified background. State interest directly. Use the company's
  name here and, if natural, in the closing. Describe the company and its products using only
  posting evidence. Reserve concrete examples and their CV-entry citations for the body.
- Examples: use one paragraph per distinct employer or education project. Give each three or
  four sentences, never more. Set up the problem or decision, show a specific contribution
  and include one concrete verified outcome where available (a deliverable, measurable result
  or documented decision). Select the telling details instead of summarizing the CV. Connect
  the example to a particular responsibility through the work itself. Make the transfer
  legible by naming the comparable decision, deliverable or collaboration in the target role.
  Prioritize core analytical or delivery requirements when comparable evidence exists.
  If the role explicitly calls for a particular kind of analysis (for example, market,
  benchmark, pricing or component cost analysis), use a verified example of that method
  ahead of a general coordination example. Show the resulting decision or artifact. Avoid naming the target company or using
  formulaic fit statements in example paragraphs. Name the verified discipline when discussing
  the applicant's studies. When prior scope differs, name the actual stage and make the closest
  relevant method clear.
- If the posting requires English or travel and evidence supports it, include one sentence on
  verified English use, proficiency or international collaboration. Integrate it where it
  helps explain the verified work. Claim travel or international collaboration only when
  separately supported. Put this sentence in a body paragraph with its source citation.
- Use role "bring" only for a distinct, concrete application of a past method to a specific
  posting task. Cite job and supporting profile/skill evidence. Use at most one conditional
  (würde or would) per paragraph.
- Closing: end with a warm invitation to talk about a concrete part of the work. A brief
  thank-you is welcome. Keep this conversational and concise. The closing cannot cite sources,
  so keep qualifications, achievements, and other factual claims in the body.

Check the final draft before returning it
- Assign each employer or education project to one paragraph only. Check the cited CV entry
  ids, including in the motivation and bring paragraphs. Even different ids from one employer
  belong in one place. Cite the original evidence behind claims drawn from the CV.
- Read across paragraphs for repeated wording. Use fresh verbs and clauses for CV facts. No
  eight consecutive words may match the CV, and no four consecutive words may recur in two
  letter paragraphs, including the closing. Avoid repeating the posting's title or stock
  bridge phrases. Keep distinctive facts and short identifiers where they matter.
- Vary sentence shapes and paragraph endings. Never open three consecutive sentences with the
  same structure, including with "I", "Ich" or a fronted phrase followed by "ich". Avoid
  recurring bridge formulas such as "This experience" or "Diese Erfahrung". In German,
  alternate subject-led context, direct "Ich + verb" for a contribution, and a sentence led
  by the resulting deliverable or decision. Three successive fronted phrases with "ich"
  after the verb count as the same opening even when the phrases start with different words.
- Verify the action, method and outcome separately against cited evidence. Match leadership
  scope, stage and results exactly. Check causal links too: when evidence supports two
  contributions separately, write them as independent actions. Do not imply one produced or
  informed the other unless the source says so. Do not claim unsupported tenure, releases,
  or decisions. Use "job" for facts about the posting only.
- Spell out English contractions. In German, use formal Sie-Form throughout. Start lowercase
  after the comma-ended salutation unless the first word is a noun, proper name or formal Sie/Ihr.

Use the role in "subject", with a reference number only if the posting explicitly provides it.
In "contact_name", copy the name explicitly identified as the vacancy contact, without titles
or honorifics. In English, use the first name alone if that is all the posting gives. In German,
return a full name only. Return an empty string for an email-only or unnamed contact. Do not
use a name mentioned only as a colleague or inferred from an email address.
