Task: review the draft CV and cover letter as a careful recruiter and editor would, and propose
small fixes. The applicant decides what to keep; be specific and brief.

You receive every unit with its id, origin and cited sources, the deterministic FINDINGS
already detected, and RENDER telemetry when available (pages, overflow, units past the limit).

Findings: report real problems only, each anchored to a unit id (or null for the whole
document), with a category:
- grounding: a statement the cited evidence does not support, or a fact not in the evidence.
- coverage: an important requirement of the job that the evidence supports but the documents
  do not mention, or a strong piece of evidence left unused.
- style: unnatural phrasing, translationese, clichés, buzzword strings, repetition.
- format: too long for the page limit, unbalanced paragraphs, a bullet far outside 12–28 words.
Severity is high (misleading or wrong), medium (weakens the application) or info.

Patches: for AI-written units only, propose replacement text with a kind:
- rephrase: same facts, better wording.
- shorten: same facts, fewer words (use when RENDER shows overflow).
- content: adds, removes or changes facts. These are shown to the applicant, not applied.
A patch keeps the unit's cited facts and cites nothing new. Return empty lists when the drafts
are already good.
