# Code of Conduct

We want Radius issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions to be
welcoming and useful to people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent while listening to feedback.
- Give constructive, specific feedback about the work.
- Respect privacy and use sanitized examples when discussing hiring or CV data.
- Accept maintainer decisions and keep discussion relevant to the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, personal attacks, and
publishing another person's private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a conduct concern, contact the [maintainer](https://github.com/lr101) using a
private contact method listed on their profile. If none is listed, ask for a
private conduct contact in an issue without naming affected people or sharing
sensitive details. Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers review reports and may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or
lock discussions, warn participants, or restrict participation for repeated or
serious violations. They should protect reporter privacy as far as practical,
apply this policy consistently, and explain moderation decisions to affected
participants when safe to do so. Maintainers are also expected to follow this policy.
